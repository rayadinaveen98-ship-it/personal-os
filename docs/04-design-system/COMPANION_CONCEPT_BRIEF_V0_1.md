# Personal OS — Companion Concept Brief v0.1

**Status:** Ready for first visual concept exploration.

## Goal
Create a calm 2D companion identity that makes Personal OS feel alive, warm and premium without making it childish or distracting.

## Explore three directions in one concept sheet

### Direction A — Soft Life Spirit
- abstract rounded little spirit / soft creature
- simple silhouette
- tiny arms/legs optional
- expressive eyes and minimal mouth
- calm, contemporary, emotionally warm
- should feel natural beside premium editorial UI

### Direction B — Tiny Observatory Friend
- small human-like but highly simplified figure
- rounded proportions, soft clothes/forms
- peaceful neutral-gender presence
- capable of sitting, waving, stretching, reading/observing
- mature-cute rather than children's-cartoon

### Direction C — Gentle Animal-Inspired Companion
- subtle animal inspiration without being an obvious existing mascot species
- rounded ears/form allowed
- quiet curious personality
- premium illustration language, not sticker/cartoon style

## Shared requirements
- Warm Personal Observatory palette: ivory/cream, moss/sage, muted gold, lavender, restrained terracotta
- clean 2D illustration
- soft low-contrast shading
- strong readable silhouette at small UI sizes
- no text baked into character artwork
- no hyperactive pose
- no huge anime eyes
- no toy/plush photorealism
- no 3D render
- no brand/IP resemblance

## Show for each direction
At minimum:
- neutral idle full-body pose
- small tap-response pose (wave/stretch/smile)
- evening/reflection variant or expression cue

## Presentation
Concept sheet should feel like a premium product character study, not a children's animation model sheet.

Neutral warm background. Clear separation between A/B/C. Character identity matters more than decorative layout.

## Selection criterion
Choose the direction that best satisfies:
1. calmness
2. premium fit with app UI
3. emotional warmth
4. recognizability at small size
5. animation simplicity
6. ability to exist in morning/evening/reflection/success contexts

Once selected, create a consistent pose/state sheet before using it in screen references.
