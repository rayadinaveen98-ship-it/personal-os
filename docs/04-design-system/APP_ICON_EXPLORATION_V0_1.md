# Personal OS — App Icon Exploration v0.1

**Status:** Concept exploration in progress

This file defines the constrained set of launcher-icon concepts that may be considered before the final adaptive icon is frozen.

## Non-negotiable principles
- launcher icon must remain simple at small Android sizes
- it must work under circle, squircle and rounded-square adaptive masks
- it must have a strong monochrome/themed-icon interpretation
- it must not use Tiny Observatory Friend as the launcher portrait
- it must feel premium, calm and timeless rather than playful-first
- the splash/launch mark should reuse the winning launcher symbol

## Concept directions

### A — Quiet Observatory
A minimal personal-observatory mark: soft circular/orbital enclosure with one focal point/horizon. Meaning: attention, perspective, a personal world.

### B — Personal Portal
A softly rounded doorway/window form suggesting entering a private life space. Meaning: this is your place, your system, your context.

### C — Life Orbit
Two or three restrained nested arcs around a small upward-growth form. Meaning: plans, memories, growth and reflection existing around one person.

### D — Observatory Horizon
A horizon/sun-or-moon line inside a simple rounded enclosure. Meaning: today, continuity, reflection and another day ahead.

## Color family
Primary exploration:
- Warm Ivory `#F5F1E8`
- Moss Sage `#667A61`
- deep Moss / Ink variant for contrast
- optional restrained Gold accent only if it remains readable in monochrome

## Rejection criteria
Reject any concept that:
- reads as a generic plant app, meditation app or calendar app
- needs tiny internal details to make sense
- becomes illegible at 48dp launcher preview size
- cannot produce a clean monochrome variant
- depends on gradients/shadows for its silhouette
- looks like a mascot avatar rather than a system brand

## Next step
Create a focused four-concept icon sheet showing each direction as:
1. primary light launcher icon
2. dark-background variant
3. monochrome/themed glyph
4. tiny launcher-size preview

One direction will then be selected and refined into Android adaptive-icon foreground/background assets plus launch/splash mark.
